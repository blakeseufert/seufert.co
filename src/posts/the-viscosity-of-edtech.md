Education technology is viscous. Once it settles into a school, it resists movement. Not because people lack imagination, but because every product becomes tangled with habits, policies, training, data, and informal workarounds.

## Switching Has a Texture

The cost of change is rarely the license fee. It is the staff meeting, the migration spreadsheet, the parent email, the timetable dependency, and the person who quietly knows how the old thing behaves on a Friday afternoon.

{% pullquote %}
Tools become infrastructure long before anyone admits they are infrastructure.
{% endpullquote %}

### Reducing Drag

1. Keep exports clean.
2. Write down the real workflows.
3. Sunset tools deliberately.

The work is less about chasing novelty and more about keeping the organisation able to move.
