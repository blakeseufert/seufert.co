Chromebooks win when the job is not to impress a spec sheet, but to keep a learning environment moving. Their best trait is operational: they reduce the number of weird edge cases a school has to carry.

## The Real Device Question

It is tempting to ask which device is best. The better question is which device produces the fewest avoidable interruptions across thousands of school days.

### What Matters

- Fast sign-in
- Simple replacement
- Predictable management
- Clear ownership boundaries

> The best school device is usually the one nobody has to think about for very long.

When the support model is simple, teachers get more of the school day back.
